Privacy Policy

Established: 12 October 2026 · Revised: 22 October 2026 (ad provider, where usage data is stored and how to delete it, cookies, what is stored on your device)

ExileDesk ("the site", https://exiledesk.com/) is an unofficial fan site for trying Path of Exile 2 crafting. This page explains how the site handles information. The Japanese version is the original; if the two differ, the Japanese version applies.

1. What we collect

Usage data (to improve the site)

  • Actions and results: currencies used, steps and outcomes, simulator settings and results
  • A random ID created per device (not linked to any name or account)
  • Site version, screen type (PC or mobile) and country (from the connection)
  • Which pages were opened (including articles and blog posts), where you came from (the previous site's name), and whether share buttons were pressed

Usage data contains no names, email addresses or IP addresses, and there are no accounts. It is sent in one batch when you leave a page (at most once an hour) and stored in Cloudflare's database (D1) to improve the site. Only the data above, the time it was sent and the country are stored; IP addresses and browser user agents are not. To delete it, use "don't log" in section 4, which deletes that device's data; for any other deletion request, please use the contact form.

Feedback and contact (only when you send it via "Send feedback" or the contact form)

  • Your message, and the current screen state (base, mods, steps) when sent from "Send feedback"
  • A reply address if you enter one in the contact form (used only to reply)
  • Your browser's user agent and IP address (to prevent repeated sending)

Feedback is deleted after 90 days and replies after 180 days.

2. How we use it

  • Improving features and investigating and fixing bugs
  • Counting visitors in a way that does not identify individuals
  • Replying to feedback (shown the next time you open the site on that device)

We do not sell or share it with third parties except where required by law.

3. Stored on your device

The site keeps the following in your browser. All of it stays on your device and reaches the site only as part of the usage data or requests you send. Clearing the browser's site data removes it.

  • Browser storage (localStorage): display settings such as language, display currency, sound on/off and which panels are open; omen settings and your mod weight edits; recipes and trees you saved in the simulator;tutorials you have finished; which changelog entries and replies to your requests you have seen; a copy of the requests you sent (the request number and the first 60 characters); the random ID for usage data, usage data waiting to be sent, and the “Don't log this device” setting. These stay until you delete them.
  • Tab storage (sessionStorage): the item you are working on and the steps you used, so that coming back from a blog post or another page shows the same screen. It is removed when you close the tab.
  • Offline cache (Service Worker): the site's pages, images and other files, so it can open without a connection.

4. Opting out

Open this link to stop sending usage data from this device and delete what was already sent. This link turns it back on.

5. Advertising and cookies

The site uses Google AdSense, a third-party advertising service. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site or other sites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this and other sites. This information does not include your name or email address.

  • You can opt out of personalized advertising in Google Ads Settings, and out of third-party vendors' cookies at aboutads.info / youronlinechoices.eu.
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

The site itself does not use cookies (your work and settings are kept in device storage as in section 3). Sponsored slots (such as streamer features) are marked "PR".

6. Third-party services

  • Cloudflare: hosting, and storing usage data (D1) and feedback.
  • Google AdSense: serving ads (section 5).
  • Google Fonts: displaying text.
  • YouTube / Twitch: streamer thumbnails and icons are loaded from their servers.
  • poe2scout: the source of market prices (fetched by our server, not directly from your device).

Each service handles information under its own privacy policy. External pages such as the trade site open only when you click a link.

7. Changes to this policy

Changes are announced on this page, and major changes also in the site's notices.

8. Contact

Use the contact form or "Send feedback" at the top right of the site.
