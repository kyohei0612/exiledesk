About ExileDesk

ExileDesk is a free fan-made tool for trying Path of Exile 2 crafting in your browser before spending real currency. You can repeat the same craft as many times as you like and see what each step does.

Open the Craft Stage

What you can do

  • Emulator: hold a currency, bone, omen, essence or Liquid Emotion and click the item to use it one step at a time. See which mods were added or removed and the chance at that moment. Undo with the R key.
  • Simulator: choose the target mods and steps, run many attempts, and get the cost per finished item and the range of luck. Save the steps as a recipe.
  • Mod list: every mod and tier for the base, with the chance to roll next using the currency you are holding.
  • Market prices: currency prices are filled in automatically to total the cost of your steps. Trade searches open in a new tab.
  • Weapons, armour, jewellery and jewels (Ruby, Emerald, Sapphire, Diamond and Time-Lost) are supported.

Chances and rules

  • Mods, tiers and weights come from game data and poe2db. Names and texts are the game client's own wording.
  • Chances are estimates from that data, not official values, and results are not guaranteed.
  • Where a rule is unclear, we check reports and videos from players who tested it before adding it. If something looks wrong, please tell us via "Send feedback".
