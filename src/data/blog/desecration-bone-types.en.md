PoE2 desecration bones come in **three slots (Jawbone, Rib, Collarbone) and three grades (Gnawed, Preserved, Ancient)**. On Ribs, the ones we checked, a Gnawed Rib only works on items up to Item Level 64, a Preserved Rib has no restriction, and an Ancient Rib adds "Minimum Modifier Level 40".

:::zundamon normal
I want the Desecrated Arcane Surge mod on my gloves. But there are so many bones that I froze.
:::

:::metan normal
The names are almost the same, yet the prices differ by orders of magnitude.
:::

:::zundamon happy
The pricier the bone, the better the mods, right?
:::

:::metan normal
Actually, on Item Level 82 Intelligence gloves, the priciest Ancient bone's restriction didn't remove a single Desecrated mod.
:::

:::zundamon surprised
Not a single one?! Then why are there different bones at all?
:::

:::summary What you'll learn
- Bones are slot-specific: Jawbone = weapons and quivers, Rib = armour, Collarbone = amulets, rings and belts
- A Gnawed Rib is capped at Item Level 64. The Ancient Rib's Minimum Modifier Level 40 doesn't remove any glove Desecrated mod (all are modifier level 65)
- Ancient bones are by far the most expensive. On Item Level 65+ gear, a Preserved bone is usually enough
:::

:::tip New to this? Words used here
- **Desecration**: A craft where a bone adds an Unrevealed mod. Revealing it decides what the mod is
- **Bone**: The currency used for desecration. Bones differ by slot and grade
- **Desecrated mod**: A mod that only desecration can add
- **Item Level**: A number on each item. It decides which bones work and which mods can roll
- **Minimum Modifier Level**: A floor. Tiers below this modifier level can't be added
- **div**: Divine Orb, used as the unit of price
:::

## Each Slot Has Its Own Bone

Bones don't work on every item. Each slot has its own bone.

:::zundamon normal
Can I use any bone on any item?
:::

:::metan normal
No. The description says what each bone works on. Here's a table.
:::

| Bone | Desecrates (item description) | For example |
|---|---|---|
| Jawbone | a Rare Weapon or Quiver | Weapons and quivers (talismans too, in ExileDesk) |
| Rib | a Rare Armour | Gloves and helmets |
| Collarbone | a Rare Amulet, Ring or Belt | Rings |

:::zundamon happy
Gloves are armour, so they take a Rib!
:::

:::metan normal
Right. Helmets take a Rib too, and rings take a Collarbone.
:::

:::zundamon normal
Do Collarbones only come in the three grades?
:::

:::metan normal
There's one more, the Altered Collarbone. Its description adds a "chance for otherworldly modifiers".
:::

:::zundamon normal
Has ExileDesk ever used the wrong bone for a slot?
:::

:::metan troubled
It has. Talismans were treated as armour, so faction omens didn't work on them.
:::

:::metan normal
v0.1.508 fixed that and gave talismans Jawbones. The same version stopped offering bones for sceptres with no Desecrated mods.
:::

## Gnawed vs Preserved vs Ancient: Only the Level Limits Differ

Within a slot, all three grades simply "desecrate". The only difference is a level restriction.

:::zundamon normal
Does a higher grade make the effect stronger?
:::

:::metan normal
Every description just says "desecrate". Only the level restriction differs. Let's line up the Ribs.
:::

| Bone | Restriction | Item Level 82 Intelligence gloves (Craft of Exile) | What it means |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gnawed Rib | Maximum Item Level 64 | **Not usable** (greyed out) | Can't be picked at Item Level 65+ |
| Preserved Rib | None | Usable | Works at any item level |
| Ancient Rib | Minimum Modifier Level 40 | Usable | Cuts tiers below modifier level 40 |

:::zundamon surprised
So a Gnawed Rib doesn't work on these gloves!
:::

:::metan normal
Craft of Exile's hover shows Maximum Item Level 64. On gear at Item Level 65 or higher, it's never an option.
:::

:::zundamon normal
Where did you confirm the Ancient Rib's 40?
:::

:::metan normal
Both poe2db and Craft of Exile show the same "Minimum Modifier Level 40".
:::

## Does the Ancient Bone's Minimum Modifier Level 40 Remove Desecrated Mods?

Not on Intelligence gloves. None of their Desecrated mods fall below the level 40 floor.

:::zundamon happy
A floor of 40 cuts the weak Desecrated mods, so it's a win!
:::

:::metan normal
On Intelligence gloves, nothing gets cut. There are 15 Desecrated mods, and **every one is modifier level 65 with a single tier**.
:::

| Intelligence glove Desecrated mods | Count | Modifier level | Tiers | With an Ancient Rib (floor 40) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Amanamu | 3 | 65 | 1 | Stays |
| Kurgal | 5 | 65 | 1 | Stays |
| Ulaman | 7 | 65 | 1 | Stays |

:::zundamon normal
Isn't that different from Greater and Perfect orbs?
:::

:::metan normal
Greater and Perfect orbs use a minimum modifier level to cut low tiers. Glove Desecrated mods have no low tiers, so there's nothing to cut.
:::

:::zundamon normal
So an Ancient bone rolls exactly the same mods?
:::

:::metan troubled
That's a separate question. Neither poe2db nor Craft of Exile says whether the grade changes the mods or their odds.
:::

:::note fold Not yet verified (3)
- Whether the bone grade changes the Desecrated mod pool or its odds is unconfirmed (neither poe2db nor Craft of Exile lists it).
- We only checked the level limits on Ribs (Intelligence gloves). The sources here don't confirm that Jawbones and Collarbones of the same grade have the same limits.
- The Gnawed Rib's Item Level 64 cap was confirmed in Craft of Exile's hover. Our research notes disagree on whether poe2db's bone page states it, so this post relies on Craft of Exile.
:::

### How Much Do Bones Cost?

Both price sources agree on one thing: Ancient bones are far more expensive than the rest.

:::zundamon normal
How big is the price gap?
:::

| Bone | poe2db in-page trade rate (2026-10-21) | ExileDesk server price (2026-10-20) | How to read it |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Gnawed Rib | 4 Chaos Orbs each | 6.39 Exalted | Cheap. Whether it's cheaper than Preserved flips between sources |
| Preserved Rib | 1 Exalted Orb buys 1.15 | 6.75 Exalted | Cheap. About the same as Gnawed on the server price |
| Ancient Rib | 2.4 div each | 4,702 Exalted | **Far more expensive** (in both sources) |

:::zundamon surprised
Only the Ancient Rib is on a whole different scale!
:::

:::metan normal
Both sources agree on that. But they disagree on whether Gnawed or Preserved is cheaper.
:::

:::metan troubled
The direction of poe2db's rates is our reading of its notation, so it's an estimate. Thinly traded items are a weak guide, so be careful.
:::

:::tip On Item Level 65+ gear, start with a Preserved bone
A Gnawed bone can't be used, and the Ancient bone's floor removes no Desecrated mods. Given the price gap, a Preserved bone is the sensible default for Item Level 65+ gear (whether the grade changes the odds is unconfirmed, so that part is a judgment call).
:::

## How ExileDesk Handles Bone Grades

The emulator's rules for bones were settled over a few versions.

| Version | What changed | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| v0.1.467 | With Preserved bones, Ancient bones and the Altered Collarbone, Desecrated-only mods appear as reveal options **regardless of item level** | Low item level gear can roll T1, which may raise the level requirement |
| v0.1.531 | Using a bone with a Mark of the Abyssal Lord sets the tier floor to "item level × 0.4" (previously fixed at 33) | The floor now matches Craft of Exile |
| v0.1.508 | Talismans take Jawbones; no bones for sceptres without Desecrated mods | Jawbones work on talismans, and sceptres don't list bones they can't use |

:::zundamon normal
Do Gnawed bones also show options regardless of item level?
:::

:::metan normal
Gnawed bones aren't part of the v0.1.467 change. They work as before.
:::

:::zundamon normal
Where does a Mark of the Abyssal Lord come from?
:::

:::metan normal
An Essence of the Abyss adds it. Only then does the tier floor become item level × 0.4.
:::

:::zundamon normal
When I use a bone, does it land on a prefix or a suffix?
:::

:::metan normal
That's a separate rule from the grade. It's covered in [Desecration and Omen of Necromancy: Which Side?](/en/blog/desecration-bone-sides/).
:::

Bones split into three slots and three grades, and the grades differ only in level limits. On Item Level 65+ gear, a Preserved bone is usually enough.
