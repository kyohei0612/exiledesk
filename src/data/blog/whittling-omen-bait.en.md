PoE2's Omen of Whittling makes a Chaos Orb **remove the mod with the lowest modifier level**. Used as-is while adding a prefix to a Gold Ring, 96.5% of 3,000 runs removed a target suffix and broke the ring. When we first put a low-level essence mod on the item as "bait", Whittling removed only the bait, and the craft came out about 80-120 div cheaper per ring than Omen of Sinistral Erasure.

:::zundamon normal
Omen of Whittling is cheaper than Omen of Sinistral Erasure. Can I just use that?
:::

:::metan troubled
On price alone, yes. But used as-is, it removes the suffix you worked for.
:::

:::zundamon surprised
It removes "the lowest" mod. How does that hit a target?
:::

:::metan normal
On this ring, the target suffix had the lowest modifier level. We had 3,000 runs try each way.
:::

:::summary What you'll learn
- Omen of Whittling removes the non-fractured mod with the **lowest modifier level**
- Using "Whittling + Perfect Chaos" as-is on a Gold Ring broke **96.5%** of runs by removing the Rarity T1 suffix (modifier level 40)
- With a low-level essence mod as bait, Whittling removes only the bait. **About 463-488 div** per ring, 15-20% cheaper than Sinistral Erasure (about 567-591 div)
- Runic Alloy, Essence of the Abyss and Essence of the Breach all work as bait. The difference is just their price
:::

:::tip New to this? Words used here
- **Omen of Whittling**: makes the next Chaos Orb remove the lowest-level mod
- **Omen of Sinistral Erasure**: makes the next Chaos Orb remove a prefix only
- **Modifier level**: the level attached to each mod tier. Stronger tiers have higher levels
- **Omen of Sinistral Crystallisation**: makes a Perfect Essence (and similar) replace a prefix only
- **Fractured**: a mod locked in place so it can't be removed
:::

## What Does Omen of Whittling Remove?

A Chaos Orb with Omen of Whittling removes the non-fractured mod with the **lowest modifier level**, then adds one. If several tie for lowest, one of them is picked evenly.

:::zundamon normal
So you can't pick a side?
:::

:::metan normal
No. Prefix or suffix, the lowest one goes. Check which mod is lowest before you use it.
:::

Here is the ring we crafted.

| Item | Details | Modifier level |
|---|---|---:|
| Base | Gold Ring, rare, item level 82 | — |
| Suffix (done) | Rarity T1 | **40** |
| Same | Cast Speed T2 | 51 |
| Same | All Elemental Resistances T2 | 54 |
| Prefix (fractured) | Spell Damage T2 (a Genesis Tree mod) | — |
| Target | Item Rarity prefix T1 | 47 |

The suffixes are already finished. The goal is to add a second target on the prefix side.

## Used As-Is, 96% Break

Adding one prefix with a plain Exalted Orb and then repeating "Whittling + Perfect Chaos" broke almost every run.

| Route | Per ring | Finished | What happens |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Sinistral Exaltation + Greater Exalt → **Sinistral Erasure + Perfect Chaos** | 567.4 / 591.0 div | 96.9% | Only prefixes get swapped |
| Plain Exalt → **Whittling + Perfect Chaos** | 909.1 / 901.6 div + rebuilds | **3.5%** | 96.5% remove the Rarity suffix |

Per-ring costs are for two random seeds. The Whittling route costs this much even when we count rebuilding a broken ring as free.

:::zundamon troubled
Why does the suffix get removed?
:::

:::metan normal
A Perfect Chaos Orb adds mods at modifier level 50 or higher, or the Rarity prefix T1 at 47. The Rarity T1 suffix is only 40.
:::

:::metan troubled
Usually the first Whittling removes the low junk prefix from the plain Exalt. After that, the Rarity T1 suffix is the lowest mod. 64% of everything removed was that Rarity suffix.
:::

## Bait Makes It the Cheapest Route

So we put an **essence mod with an even lower modifier level** on the prefix side before using Whittling. Whittling always removes the bait and never touches the suffixes.

| One cycle | What you use | Cost per cycle |
|---|---|---:|
| Erasure route | Omen of Sinistral Erasure + Perfect Chaos | 27.2 div |
| Bait route (Runic Alloy as bait) | Omen of Whittling + Perfect Chaos + Omen of Sinistral Crystallisation + Runic Alloy | 21.6 div |

Both routes hit the target about 5% of the time per cycle. With Runic Alloy the bait route is 5.6 div cheaper per cycle, and that's most of the difference.

| Route (3,000 runs, 2 seeds) | Per ring | Finished | Which is better |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Sinistral Erasure + Perfect Chaos | 567.4 / 591.0 div | 96.9 / 96.7% | Baseline |
| Bait = Runic Alloy | **462.6 / 468.1 div** | 98.8 / 99.0% | About 105-123 div (about 20%) cheaper |
| Bait = Essence of the Abyss | 472.3 / 466.3 div | 98.7 / 98.6% | About 95-125 div cheaper (same as Alloy within the margin) |
| Bait = Essence of the Breach | 487.7 / 484.4 div | 98.4 / 98.1% | About 80-107 div cheaper (the bait costs more) |

:::zundamon happy
Just adding bait makes it 15-20% cheaper!
:::

:::metan normal
Here's the loop. When a junk prefix lands, swap it for bait with Omen of Sinistral Crystallisation + the bait essence. Then Whittling + Perfect Chaos removes the bait and adds one mod. If it misses, turn the new junk back into bait and repeat.
:::

## Which Essences Work as Bait?

Bait must roll on a ring prefix and have a lower modifier level than the lowest target suffix (Rarity T1, level 40).

| Bait | Mod it adds | Modifier level | Price (2026-10-21) |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Runic Alloy | Runic Ward | 13 | 184.5 exalts |
| Essence of the Abyss | Mark of the Abyssal Lord | 1 | 221.4 exalts |
| Essence of the Breach | Maximum quality | 1 | 867.7 exalts |

Each run used about 20 bait essences. The three baits differ almost only by price.

:::tip Before you use Whittling
Check which mod on the item has the lowest modifier level. If it's one of your targets, put a lower-level bait on first.
:::

:::note fold What we haven't checked (3)
- Costs come from the engine, not from crafting hundreds of rings in the live game. The Whittling and Perfect Chaos rules have been checked against Craft of Exile.
- The starting ring's suffixes were set at the lowest target tier (T2). Higher tiers have higher modifier levels, so Whittling still removes only the bait.
- Prices are ExileDesk market prices (2026-10-21 17:00 UTC, Forbidden Rites, 1 div = 807.07 exalts). If omen or essence prices move, the cheapest route can change.
- Rebuilding rings broken by Whittling, and making the starting ring (three suffixes plus the fracture), are not included.
:::

Omen of Whittling removes the lowest-level mod, so it breaks your craft when a target is the lowest. Put a lower-level bait on first, and it beats Sinistral Erasure by about 15-20%.
