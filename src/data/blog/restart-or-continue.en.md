Halfway through crafting the suffixes on a PoE2 Gold Ring, should you start over? We counted it: **a plain Annulment, a Chaos Orb back to the start, or a brand-new base all cost about 200 div per ring**. Only Omen of Dextral Annulment came out worse, by about the price of the omen (around 13 div). Even holding two targets, you're only 0-20 div ahead of starting fresh. All of this is for this ring, with a single fractured prefix, and these crafting rules.

:::summary What you'll learn
- From Cast Speed T2 + one junk mod, a plain Annul, a Dextral Exalt, a Chaos back to start, and restarting all cost **about 197-201 div**
- Only Omen of Dextral Annulment + Annul costs **about 13 div more**. With no removable prefix, the omen does nothing useful
- With two targets + one high-tier junk mod, a plain Annul is the cheapest or tied. You're only 0-20 div ahead of starting fresh
- Omen of Whittling can pay off when the junk mod's modifier level is lower than your targets (1 of the 3 cases we tested)
:::

:::tip New to this? Words used here
- **Plain Annul**: an Orb of Annulment with no omen. Removes one non-fractured mod evenly
- **Omen of Dextral Annulment**: makes the next Annulment remove a suffix only
- **Omen of Whittling**: makes the next Chaos Orb remove the lowest-level mod
- **Modifier level**: the level attached to each mod tier. Stronger tiers have higher levels
- **Fractured**: a mod locked in place so it can't be removed
:::

## What Ring Did We Compare?

A Gold Ring at item level 82, partway to three target suffixes.

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| Prefix | 33% Spell Damage (T2), fractured. Nothing else |
| Targets (suffixes) | Rarity T1, Cast Speed T2+, All Elemental Resistances T2+ |
| Example junk | Dexterity T3 (modifier level 55). Also T6 (modifier level 22) for comparison |
| From the 2nd craft on | 0 targets → Chaos Orb; junk → plain Annul right away; no Rarity → Omen of Dextral Exaltation + Greater Exalted Orb; Rarity → catalyst (20% quality) + Omen of Catalysing Exaltation + Omen of Dextral Exaltation + Perfect Exalted Orb |

Only the first craft changes. From the second craft on, every run follows the same rules to the end. Prices are ExileDesk market prices (2026-10-21).

## One Cast Speed: All the Same Except Dextral Annulment

From Cast Speed T2 + one junk mod, 10,000 runs each:

| First craft | Per ring (2 seeds) | How to read it |
|---|---:|---|
| Plain Annul | 200.3 / 197.0 div | Baseline |
| Dextral Exalt to add the 3rd mod first | 200.4 / 197.3 div | Same |
| Chaos Orb back to the start | 200.7 / 197.3 div | Same |
| New base from scratch | 200.4 / 197.3 div | Same (base and fracture not included) |
| Omen of Dextral Annulment + Annul | 213.5 / 210.2 div | **About 13 div worse** |

Apart from Dextral Annulment, everything is within 0.5 div.

## Two Targets: A Plain Annul Is Nearly Always Cheapest

We also started from two targets + one junk mod, with suffixes full (5,000 runs × 2 seeds averaged, margin ±5 div).

| Two targets held | Plain Annul | Chaos back to start | Dextral Annulment + Annul | Whittling + Chaos (junk T3 / T6) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Rarity + Cast | **185.8 div** | 188.8 | 199.0 | 212.6 / 185.7 |
| Rarity + Resists | **178.2 div** | 183.4 | 191.4 | 212.0 / **169.3** |
| Cast + Resists | **196.1 div** | 196.9 | 209.3 | 212.0 / 206.9 |

With T3 junk, a plain Annul was cheapest or tied. Only with T6 junk and Rarity + Resists did Whittling + Chaos come out about 9 div cheaper. The runs share the same random sequence across moves, so the gaps are firmer than the ±5 div margin, and the direction of each gap was the same on both seeds.

:::zundamon normal
Even with two targets, it's only 0-20 div off the ~200 div fresh start.
:::

:::metan normal
Most of the cost in these rules is annulling away a target and going back to Chaos. So on this ring, a half-done item is worth less than it feels.
:::

## Whittling Can Help When the Junk Is Low

Omen of Whittling removes the lowest-level mod. The targets' modifier levels are Rarity T1 = 40, Cast Speed T2 = 51 and All Elemental Resistances T2 = 54.

| Junk | Junk modifier level | Whittling removes | Result |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Dexterity T3 | 55 | A target (lower than the junk) | Worse than a plain Annul (about 212-213 div) |
| Dexterity T6 | 22 | The junk (lowest) | About 9 div cheaper than a plain Annul, only for Rarity + Resists. No gain otherwise |

Whittling reliably removes the junk only when the junk is lower than every target. We covered Whittling in more depth in [Omen of Whittling Needs Bait](/en/blog/whittling-omen-bait/).

:::tip When there's nothing to remove on the prefix side
If, like this ring, your prefix side is just one fractured mod, don't get attached to a half-done ring. Continuing with a plain Annul, no omen, was the cheapest or tied. When targets sit on a side with no junk, omens can pay off instead ([Omens Worth It?](/en/blog/are-omens-worth-it/)).
:::

:::note fold What we haven't checked (4)
- Costs come from the engine, not from crafting hundreds of rings in the live game.
- Prices are ExileDesk market prices (2026-10-21 17:00 UTC, 1 div = 807.07 exalts). If omen prices move, the gaps move too.
- Restarting does not include a new Gold Ring or the fracture. Counting those makes restarting more expensive.
- [Annul Misses Early](/en/blog/dont-hoard-misses/) gives 220 div for the same rules. That used 2026-10-20 prices (1 div = 696.93 exalts); in exalts the two agree within the 300-run margin.
:::

While crafting suffixes on this Gold Ring, every next move except Dextral Annulment costs about the same. With nothing removable on the prefix side, keep going with a plain Annul when in doubt.
