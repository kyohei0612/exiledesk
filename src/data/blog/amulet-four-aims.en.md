Crafting four targets on a PoE2 Stellar Amulet cost **over 100 times more or less depending on the order and the crafts you pick**. Finish the suffixes first, pull Energy Shield with a catalyst, and pick Spirit from a bone's three options: about 700 div per amulet, with 99% of runs finishing. A naive "annul junk, use Greater Exalts" approach cost about 90,000 div, and only 2% finished.

:::summary What you'll learn
- The cheapest route costs **about 701-712 div** per amulet, and 99% of runs finish
- Just annulling junk and using Greater Exalts costs **about 90,000 div**, and 2% finish
- Two things make it cheap: clear suffix junk with a **plain Annul** while the prefixes are still empty, and on the prefix side (which has no room for junk) pull ES with a **catalyst** and pick Spirit from a **bone's three options**
- Most of the cost is plain Annuls, Ancient Collarbones, Omens of Light and Omens of Sinistral Annulment
:::

:::tip New to this? Words used here
- **Spirit**: the stat that lets you keep skills reserved. Rolls as an amulet prefix
- **Omen of Catalysing Exaltation**: uses catalyst quality to make matching mods more likely
- **Carapace Catalyst**: adds quality for armour, evasion and Energy Shield (ES) mods
- **Ancient Collarbone**: a Desecration bone. When you reveal, you choose one of three options
- **Omen of Light**: makes the next Annulment remove only the Desecrated mod
:::

## What Amulet Did We Craft?

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| Base | Stellar Amulet, rare, item level 82 |
| Start | Spell Damage T1 (prefix), fractured |
| Targets (prefix) | Energy Shield T3+, Spirit T2+ |
| Targets (suffix) | Spell Skill Level T3+, Cast Speed T3+ |

The prefix side has two open slots besides the fracture, and the two targets fill them exactly. There's no room for junk. The suffix side has three open slots, so one junk mod can sit there.

:::zundamon normal
So any junk prefix gets in the way right away.
:::

:::metan normal
Right. The key is a route that never puts junk on the prefix side.
:::

## The Cheapest Route: About 700 div

The cheapest route had three stages (2,000 runs × 2 seeds).

| Stage | Craft | If junk lands |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Two suffixes | Omen of Dextral Exaltation + Greater Exalted Orb | When suffix junk reaches 2, plain Annul (prefixes are empty, so only suffixes can be removed) |
| 2. ES | Carapace Catalyst (quality 20) + Omen of Catalysing Exaltation + Omen of Sinistral Exaltation + Greater Exalted Orb | Omen of Sinistral Annulment + Annul (only the junk is removed, if Spirit isn't on yet) |
| 3. Spirit | Ancient Collarbone + Omen of Sinistral Necromancy → pick Spirit T2 on reveal | Omen of Light + Annul (removes only the Desecrated mod) |

| Route | Per amulet | Finished |
|---|---:|---:|
| **The three stages above** | **711.9 / 701.3 div** | 99% |
| Same, without the ES catalyst | 815.1 div | 97% |
| Annul any junk, otherwise Greater Exalt | 89,857 div | 2% |

The three-stage route used 2,000 runs × 2 seeds; the other two used 2,000 runs × 1 seed.

## Why This Order Is Cheap

Two reasons.

| Trick | What it does | What it avoids |
|---|---|---|
| Finish suffixes first | While prefixes are empty, even a plain Annul removes only suffix junk | Pricey side-picking omens (Sinistral Annulment 20.9 div, Dextral Annulment 13.4 div) |
| Roll fewer prefixes | Pull ES with a catalyst, and pick Spirit from a bone's three options | Exalting over and over on a side with no room for junk |

Carapace Catalysts are cheap (20 for 0.13 div) and pull ES. Spirit has no tag to pull, so it gets picked from the bone's three reveal options.

:::metan normal
An item can only have one Desecrated mod. So the bone is used once, for Spirit.
:::

## What If You Change the Order or Omens?

We compared other routes at 1,000 runs (1 seed).

| Change | Per amulet | What happens |
|---|---:|---|
| Preserved Collarbone instead | 751 div | Slightly more |
| Sibilant Catalyst + Omen of Catalysing Exaltation on suffixes | 934 div | Exalts drop to 1/10, but each run needs about 1,000 catalysts (430 div) |
| Spirit bone before ES | 1,691-1,790 div | Only 70% finish |
| Prefixes first | 1,623-11,955 div | Spreads widely with Exalt type and how junk is cleared |
| Bones on both sides | 13,281 div | The most expensive of these five |

:::zundamon normal
Swapping a single step more than doubles the cost.
:::

:::metan happy
The more targets you have, the more the cost depends on which side you finish first and how you clear junk.
:::

Per run, the cheapest route used about 620 Dextral Exaltation omens, 630 plain Annuls, 140 Carapace Catalysts, 6 Omens of Sinistral Annulment, 15 Ancient Collarbones and 14 Omens of Light. The big costs were plain Annuls (about 260 div), Ancient Collarbones (about 140 div), Omens of Light (about 135 div) and Sinistral Annulment (about 125 div).

:::tip With many targets
On the side with no room for junk, roll fewer times: use catalysts and bone choices. On the side that can hold junk, clear it with a plain Annul while the other side is still empty.
:::

:::note fold What we haven't checked (4)
- Costs come from the engine. We have not compared them with an outside answer (live game crafts or another tool). Different seeds moved the result by 1.5%.
- Prices are ExileDesk market prices (2026-10-22 04:00 UTC). If omen or bone prices move, the cheapest route can change.
- We have not yet built the three-stage route as a simulator tree and run it there.
- Each run is capped at 2,000 div and 20,000 crafts, and capped runs are included in the per-amulet cost. The starting amulet (with the fracture) is not included.
:::

Four targets on a Stellar Amulet cost about 700 div with suffixes first, then a catalyst, then a bone. Next to the naive 90,000 div, order and craft choice decide almost the whole bill.
